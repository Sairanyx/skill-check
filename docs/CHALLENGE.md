# The challenge

**Rethinking Recruitment**, by the City of Espoo, Junction 2026.

> How can recruitment and matchmaking events organised by the City of Espoo help companies and talent find and connect with each other, despite AI-generated job search materials and AI-assisted recruitment processes becoming common?

## What Espoo sees today

- Companies find it hard to describe their needs, and describe them late.
- Organisers receive many applications but cannot see real capabilities.
- Companies get overwhelmed by irrelevant approaches at events.
- CVs and messages are written by AI and read by AI, so decisions rest on synthetic material.
- Solutions must not disadvantage people by nationality, language, education or career path.

**Ground rules:** synthetic data only, minimal personal data, no automated decisions about people, privacy in line with the EU AI Act.

## How SkillCheck answers each criterion

| Criterion | Weight | SkillCheck |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact and relevance** | 45% | Companies describe one real task, jobseekers show the skill in a short supervised check, and companies meet the people who show it at the event. It follows how Espoo already works: Espoo presents candidates, the employer decides. |
| **Innovation and user experience** | 35% | No CV, no cover letter. A conversation about a real task with a panel of three Gemini 4 models, in Finnish, Swedish or English, by voice or text, in rooms Espoo already has. |
| **Responsible and feasible execution** | 20% | Gemma removes personal details in Finland before any other model reads an answer. People decide, every access is logged, data stays in Finland. Runs on existing spaces and a working platform. |
