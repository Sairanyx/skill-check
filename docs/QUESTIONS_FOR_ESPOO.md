# Questions for the City of Espoo mentors

Bring this to the Espoo booth or Discord (International Working Life Services and Business Services teams). Short answers are enough. Each answer either confirms a design choice or tells us what to change.

## How it works today
1. When Employment Services do a **free candidate search** for a company, how do staff decide who to present? CV, interview, gut feeling, a test?
2. How many candidates do you usually present per opening, and how long does one search take?
3. Which **event platform** runs TalentMatch, and how are profiles "pre-screened" there?
4. Roughly how many **staff hours** does one recruitment event take, from company outreach to follow-up?
5. What happens **after** an event? Do you know how many meetings led to a hire?

## Our design choices (confirm or correct)
6. In our design **the City decides by default** who is accepted for a company, and the company can also decide for itself. Is that realistic for your staff's workload and mandate?
7. Companies only see an **alias and evidence**, never names or contact details unless the jobseeker shares them. Would companies accept that? Would your team?
8. Skill checks happen **in person on City computers in testing rooms** (like university EXAM rooms). Could **libraries (Sello, Lippulaiva, Leppävaara)** or **Espoo Talent Hub** host a few computers for this?
9. Is **30 minutes** a reasonable time to ask of jobseekers? What accessibility needs should we plan for (extra time, assistive tech, languages)?
10. Our event meetings are now **15 minutes**, like TalentMatch. Is that right for your events?
11. Companies **apply to an event**, the City approves them, and they confirm **at least one week before**. Does that match how you work with companies now?

## Responsible AI and law
12. Who in the City would need to approve an AI tool like this (data protection officer, legal, IT security)?
13. Is there an existing **City AI policy** or register we should follow (for example a public algorithm register)?
14. Would the City want the AI models to run **only in Finland** (our "sovereign mode"), or is an EU cloud acceptable?
15. How long may skill-check results be kept? We assumed **12 months**.

## Value
16. What would make this worth a pilot for you: fewer no-shows, more hires, fewer staff hours, more satisfied companies?
17. Which groups do you most want to reach (for example international professionals, young people, career changers)?
18. Is there a real upcoming event where a small pilot could be tried?
